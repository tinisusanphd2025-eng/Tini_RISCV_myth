# RV_D3SK4_L1 – Introduction to Validity and Its Advantages

**Day 3 · Digital Logic with TL-Verilog and Makerchip**  
**Sub-kit D3SK4: Validity**

## Overview

A `$valid` signal marks which pipeline transactions carry real data.

## Key Points

- Benefits: easier debug, cleaner design, automatic clock gating.
- `?$valid` creates a 'when' scope – logic only meaningful when valid.
