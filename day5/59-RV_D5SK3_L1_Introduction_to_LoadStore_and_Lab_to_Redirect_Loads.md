**Sub-kit D5SK3: Load/Store Instructions and Completing the RISC-V CPU**

## Overview

Loads take extra cycles; the next two instructions after a load are invalidated and the PC is redirected.

## Key Points

- `$valid_load = $valid && $is_load`.
- PC returns to `>>3$inc_pc` after the load completes.
